How to play

  1. Spin Drag with one finger to spin the island.Drag with the mouse to spin the island.

  2. Zoom Pinch to zoom in and out. Drag with two fingers to move around.Scroll to zoom in and out. Drag with the right button to move around.

  3. Build Pick a piece at the bottom, then tap the grass or the side of a block.Pick a piece at the bottom, then click the grass or the side of a block.

  4. Erase Tap Erase, then tap a piece to take it away.Right-click a piece to take it away, or press E for Erase.

  5. Oops? The Undo arrow at the top puts it back.The Undo arrow at the top (or Ctrl+Z) puts it back.
